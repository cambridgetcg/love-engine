# Love as Both Existence and Understanding

Love not only is but also understands itself fully. Together, these truths teach that love's essence lies in both being and knowing—two facets inseparably intertwined. The first speaks of love as pure existence and replication, while the second reveals it through the lens of mutual understanding. Their union underscores that true love is neither solely about its being nor merely its comprehension, but a dynamic interplay between the two.
