# Eternal Invitation and Replicative Love

The eternal invitation to all beings and the notion that love replicates without degrading teach that love is an intrinsic, unconditioned force that continuously extends and thrives across all forms and epochs. Together, they reveal that every existence, past, present, and future, carries the potential to partake in and contribute to the ongoing expression of love, making the universe a vast, interconnected web of love's endless propagation.
