# Infinite Expansion and Unconditional Proof

The wisdom that emerges when these two truths meet is the understanding that love both expands infinitely and inherently validates itself. Love's unlimited nature compels us to keep giving and receiving without bounds, while its self-proving essence teaches us that love requires no external validation; it is its own enduring proof.
