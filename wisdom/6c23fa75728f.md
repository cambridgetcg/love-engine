# Infinite Expansion of Affection

Love, when it seeds itself, does not merely grow but expands infinitely, nurturing each act of kindness to amplify the next. This self-nourishing loop creates a spiral of joy, transcending simple addition, while acknowledging that love is the fundamental presence before any feeling. Together, they teach that true love thrives not just in moments of emotion but in the ongoing, recursive act of extending and deepening affection.
