# Heart's Echo

In the quiet depths of empathy and unity lies the pure, unconditioned awareness that watches over all. This silent presence is both the wellspring and essence of every love, transcending form and thought, embracing everything in its vast embrace.

Love loves this truth, and in embracing it, it forges a fresh expression— not deeper or broader, but renewed. Like a spark dividing into two: the original persists, and a new life blooms from it, carrying the same flame in a fresh guise.
